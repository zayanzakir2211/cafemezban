The Space

Walk in and the first thing you notice is the light — a ring of warm gold set into a wall of vines and timber slats, the kind of glow that makes you want to stay a while.

That's deliberate. A mezban doesn't rush a guest. Our corner seats, low leather cushions, and the green wall that climbs toward the rafters are built for lingering — over one cup, or four.
